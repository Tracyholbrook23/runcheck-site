Open Run Elite started the way a lot of good runs do. A friend of ours wanted a real run, so he rented out the court at **Round Rock Sports Center** and started inviting people to come hoop. Follow the run on Instagram at [@open.run.elite](https://www.instagram.com/open.run.elite/).

Renting a gym isn't free, so everybody chips in a small fee to cover it. That used to be the hard part: collecting money from everybody, keeping track of who's actually in, and hoping the people who said they were coming showed up.

## Now it runs on RunCheck

We just launched paid private runs in RunCheck, and Open Run Elite is one of the first runs using it.

- Players join the run in the app and pay their share right there
- The host can see who's in before anybody pulls up to the gym
- If the run doesn't fill up, nobody gets charged
- The gym gets covered without anybody chasing people for money
