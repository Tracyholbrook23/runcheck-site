Every hooper knows the feeling. You drive across town, lace up, walk in, and the gym is empty. Or the run already broke up. Or it's so packed you won't get on until midnight.

RunCheck fixes that. Open the app and you can see which gyms are active, how many people are checked in right now, and when runs are starting, before you ever leave the house.

## What you can do

- Find runs near you and see how many players are there right now
- Check in when you walk in, so everybody knows the gym is live
- Start a run, set the time, and get your crew to pull up
- Chat with your runs and see where your friends are playing

It's free to download, and we're starting in Austin and Round Rock. [Get RunCheck on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/runcheck-pickup-basketball/id6760801659).

If your gym isn't on the map yet, [tell us about it](/contact) and we'll add it.
