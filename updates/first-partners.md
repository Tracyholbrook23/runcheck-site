RunCheck is free for every player, and local businesses are a big part of how we keep it that way. Our first two partners are now live in the app.

## Chill-Wil Air

Commercial and residential HVAC and electrical services throughout Texas. Comfort you can afford, service you can trust. [Visit Chill-Wil Air](https://chillwilair.com)

## D-Choice Retail

Groceries, household essentials, beauty products, electronics and more at competitive prices. [Visit D-Choice Retail](https://d-choiceretail.com)

## Where you'll see them

Partners show up in the **Our Partners** section on the RunCheck home screen, the first thing players see when they open the app to find a run. Each one has its own page with their info and buttons to their website, phone and Instagram.
